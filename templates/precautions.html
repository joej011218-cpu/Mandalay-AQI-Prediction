CPCB Air Quality Categories

Health impacts and practical precautions for each AQI range.
GoodAQI 0–50
HEALTH IMPACT

Minimal impact. Air quality is considered good for normal daily activities.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Normal outdoor activities can continue.
  • Outdoor exercise and recreation can continue as usual.
  • Continue normal daily activities.
SatisfactoryAQI 51–100
HEALTH IMPACT

Air quality is generally acceptable. Sensitive people may experience minor breathing discomfort.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Normal activities can continue for most people.
  • Sensitive people should monitor for breathing discomfort.
  • Reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor exertion if discomfort occurs.
ModerateAQI 101–200
HEALTH IMPACT

Breathing discomfort may occur in people with lung, asthma or heart conditions, especially during prolonged exposure.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Sensitive groups should consider reducing prolonged or strenuous outdoor activity.
  • Take additional breaks during outdoor physical activity.
  • Move indoors if breathing discomfort occurs.
PoorAQI 201–300
HEALTH IMPACT

Prolonged exposure may cause breathing discomfort, particularly for people with respiratory or heart conditions.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Reduce prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical activity.
  • Sensitive groups should minimize outdoor exertion.
  • Consider moving strenuous activities indoors.
Very PoorAQI 301–400
HEALTH IMPACT

Prolonged exposure can cause respiratory illness or significant breathing discomfort.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Avoid strenuous outdoor physical activity.
  • Sensitive groups should remain indoors as much as practical.
  • Keep outdoor exposure as short as reasonably possible.
SevereAQI 401–500
HEALTH IMPACT

Air quality may affect healthy people and can seriously affect people with existing respiratory or cardiovascular conditions.

PRECAUTIONS
  • Avoid outdoor physical activity where possible.
  • Minimize outdoor exposure, especially for sensitive groups.
  • Move strenuous activities indoors.
AQI ranges follow the CPCB category structure used by this dashboard. The Mandalay CPCB-style estimate is calculated from Open-Meteo CAMS Global modeled pollutant data; it is not an official CPCB monitoring-station reading. Precaution text is general guidance and is not individualized medical advice.
